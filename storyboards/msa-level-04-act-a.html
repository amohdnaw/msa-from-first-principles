Shot 01

One width, no verdict

Beat 1
Persistent object
The amber gauge band, width drawn to scale from GAUGE_SIGMA. It is created in this shot and stays on screen for the whole act.
Learner prediction
none. The object has to exist before a question about it can mean anything.
Transformation
Repeated readings of one part land on a rail one at a time, then collapse into a single band. The band is the result of measuring, not a rectangle that was drawn.
Surprise
none, deliberately. This shot is the anchor the rest of the act argues against, and a surprise here would compete with beat 3.
Counterexample
none.
Formula reveal
none. No symbol is on screen. The quantity exists only as a width.
Camera move
Static wide, framed from the start for the widest denominator the act will show, so the amber band never has to be resized later. The collapse of the readings is the only motion.
Narration line
"Three levels of work produced one width. There is no verdict inside it."
Exit state
One amber band on the centre rail, tagged GAUGE_SIGMA, with nothing on screen to compare it against.
Shot 02

Commit before dividing

Prediction gate
Persistent object
The same band, moved to the left rail at unchanged width.
Learner prediction
The page asks whether one unchanged gauge can earn opposite actions. The answer is recorded and affects no access and no score.
Transformation
Two empty denominator slots open above and below the band. Each slot is an empty length with no number in it.
Surprise
The numerator slot is already full and cannot be edited. The only thing the learner can change is what sits underneath.
Counterexample
none.
Formula reveal
A fraction skeleton only: GAUGE_SIGMA on top, an empty length underneath, no bar value.
Camera move
Hold. A small vertical pull opens room above and below the band without changing its size on screen.
Narration line
"Answer now, before any arithmetic: can the same gauge be right to keep and right to replace?"
Exit state
The prediction pinned in the top corner for the rest of the act. Both denominator slots still empty.
Shot 03

The parts arrive

Beat 2, first half
Persistent object
The gauge band, same width and same screen pixels as shot 01.
Learner prediction
Does the amber band change when the parts change?
Transformation
A wider band grows behind it, built from the part spread through observed_sigma(GAUGE_SIGMA, PART_SIGMA), so the study spread is a length that contains the gauge band.
Surprise
The gauge band does not move at all, and yet the ratio it belongs to has already changed. The change came from behind it.
Counterexample
none yet. Shot 04 supplies the length that knows nothing about parts.
Formula reveal
none. study_ratio is named only as the share of the outer length the amber band covers, with no printed value.
Camera move
Hold. The pull-back that would normally fit a growing object is refused, because it shrinks the persistent object and breaks the claim.
Narration line
"Here is one thing you could hold the gauge against: how much these parts differ from each other."
Exit state
Two nested lengths. The amber band inside the study spread, with the lower denominator slot filled and no percentage on screen.
Shot 04

The drawing arrives

Beat 2, second half
Persistent object
The gauge band, unchanged again, now sitting between two hard boundaries.
Learner prediction
Do the boundaries know anything about the parts?
Transformation
Two boundaries drop in at the gap given by TOLERANCE, then slide wider and narrower while no part, no reading, and no band moves.
Surprise
The boundaries travel a long way with nothing else on screen responding. The drawing is an independent length that arrived from a decision, not a measurement.
Counterexample
The study spread stays exactly where it was while the boundary sweeps across it, so the two candidate denominators cannot be answering the same question.
Formula reveal
none. tolerance_ratio is named only as the share of the boundary gap the amber band covers.
Camera move
Hold. The boundary sweep is the only motion, so a camera move would read as the boundary moving less than it does.
Narration line
"This one came off a drawing. Nobody measured a part to get it."
Exit state
Three lengths in frame: GAUGE_SIGMA, the study spread, and the TOLERANCE gap. Both denominator slots full, still no percentage.
Shot 05

Same numerator, two questions

Beat 2 closes
Persistent object
The gauge band, copied once into each fraction at identical width, as the shared numerator.
Learner prediction
Which of the two divisions gives the larger number, and why.
Transformation
Each fraction fills its denominator with the length earned in shot 03 or shot 04, then resolves to a readout.
Surprise
The two numerator copies are visibly identical, so the difference between the two results cannot have been caused by the gauge.
Counterexample
none. This shot states the pair; shot 06 breaks it.
Formula reveal
The first symbols of the act, after both quantities exist as lengths: study_ratio(gauge=GAUGE_SIGMA, part=PART_SIGMA) and tolerance_ratio(gauge=GAUGE_SIGMA, tolerance=TOLERANCE), read out from STUDY_PCT and TOL_PCT.
Camera move
Hold, then a short push onto the two denominators. The push lands on the lengths, never on the digits.
Narration line
"One numerator, two denominators, two questions: can it sort these parts, and can it decide conformance."
Exit state
Two readouts side by side, both fed by one amber band, with each denominator still drawn to scale beneath its own ratio.
Shot 06

Improve the process, ruin the number

Beat 3
Persistent object
The gauge band, width still from GAUGE_SIGMA, with a ghost outline of its shot 01 position held behind it for the whole move.
Learner prediction
The process is about to get better. Which readout moves, and in which direction?
Transformation
The part spread animates from TIGHTEN_FROM to TIGHTEN_TO. The study band contracts toward the amber band, study_ratio climbs from STUDY_BEFORE to STUDY_AFTER, and tolerance_ratio holds at TOL_UNCHANGED.
Surprise
The process improved and %GRR_study got worse, past REJECT_PCT. The other readout did not move by a pixel.
Counterexample
Run the move backwards. Loosening the process recovers %GRR_study with nothing done to the instrument, so that ratio is reporting the parts rather than the gauge.
Formula reveal
No new formula. The same two symbols stay on screen and only the lengths move, so the arithmetic is visibly not the thing that changed.
Camera move
Hold, with a slow push on the tolerance_ratio readout precisely because it does not move. The push makes stillness legible.
Narration line
"The gauge did not get worse. The question got harder."
Exit state
The study band nearly as narrow as the amber band, STUDY_AFTER above REJECT_PCT, TOL_UNCHANGED exactly where it started, and the ghost outline still matching the live band.
Shot 07

The flip point belongs to the parts

Beat 3 extends
Persistent object
The gauge band, unchanged, now the fixed height of the amber marker on a part-spread axis.
Learner prediction
One of the two lines about to be drawn is flat. Which one?
Transformation
The part spread sweeps across the axis and both ratios draw themselves. tolerance_ratio draws a flat line at TOL_UNCHANGED. study_ratio draws a falling curve.
Surprise
A printed gate gets crossed by moving parts alone. The curve passes REJECT_PCT and ACCEPT_PCT without anyone touching the instrument.
Counterexample
The flat tolerance_ratio line, which crosses nothing across the whole sweep, so a gate crossing is not evidence about the gauge.
Formula reveal
The two gate lines ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT land on the drawn curve, and the crossings are labelled as part spreads from BAND["part_at_10"] and BAND["part_at_30"] rather than as percentages.
Camera move
Hold wide. The axis is the whole frame and the sweep supplies all the motion.
Narration line
"There is a part spread that decides the study verdict, and it is a fact about the parts."
Exit state
One flat line, one falling curve, two gate lines with their crossings named as spreads, and the amber band still at its shot 01 width in the corner.
Shot 08

The same gauge, after all of it

Beat 4
Persistent object
The gauge band, back at its shot 01 position and width, alone in frame again.
Learner prediction
The shot 02 prediction returns and is settled against what the learner has now watched.
Transformation
The study band, the boundaries, the curve, the gate lines, and both readouts drain away. The amber band is given no animation at all, so it is the one thing visibly not touched.
Surprise
Nothing was ever done to the instrument. Every number that moved during the act was a denominator.
Counterexample
The shot 01 ghost is laid over the live band at zero offset, so a viewer looking for a change to point at cannot find one.
Formula reveal
The numerator GAUGE_SIGMA shown once with the two denominator names beneath it, Study variation and Tolerance, and no percentage on screen. The act ends on the two denominators.
Camera move
Slow rise, so the drained frame reads as emptied rather than cut.
Narration line
"One instrument, two questions. The percentage belongs to the question."
Exit state
The GAUGE_SIGMA band plus the labels Study variation and Tolerance. No third readout, no verdict, no action. Act B opens on this exact frame.

Standing rules for the cut

Checks the Manim work inherits

These are read as acceptance conditions on the rendered act, not as suggestions for the animator.

Muted read

With audio off, a reviewer must be able to say: the amber band never changed, the lengths under it did, and the percentage followed the length. Any shot that needs narration to make that link is rejected.

One number path

Every geometry and every readout comes from msalab.against_what: GAUGE_SIGMA, PART_SIGMA, TOLERANCE, study_ratio, tolerance_ratio, STUDY_PCT, TOL_PCT, TIGHTEN_FROM, TIGHTEN_TO, STUDY_BEFORE, STUDY_AFTER, TOL_UNCHANGED, ACCEPT_PCT, REJECT_PCT, and BAND. No value is typed into the scene.

Colour policy

use keeps the teal accent and replace keeps the alarm accent. The band between ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT stays neutral ink, because improve is a next action and not a third light. Act A shows no action at all.

Gate

Act A is locked when all four required beats appear in this order, no shot contains ndc, the amber band has the same width in shot 01 and shot 08, and the muted read above survives a reviewer who has not read this page. Act B storyboard: msa-level-04-act-b.html.
