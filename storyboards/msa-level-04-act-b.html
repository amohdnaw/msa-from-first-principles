MSA Level 4 · visual-depth pilot · Act B shot lock

One unchanged gauge earns opposite honest actions, because the decision job chooses the denominator.

Act B takes Act A's closing frame, the same amber band, and walks it into two masked plants. Nothing is calibrated, adjusted, or replaced between them. The act's job is to turn the denominator from an interesting fact into a plant action, then show that ndc is that same ratio wearing a different label. Both printed gates land on one computed curve, so they are compared rather than memorised.

Shot 01

The frame Act A left

Carry-in
Persistent object
The identical amber gauge band, imported from Act A's closing frame at the same width and the same position, still drawn from GAUGE_SIGMA.
Learner prediction
none. The carry-in has to be checkable by eye before anything is asked of it.
Transformation
Act A's two denominator labels fade out and the band alone is lifted onto a plant rail. A ruler is laid against it, so the width is measured on screen rather than claimed in a title.
Surprise
none. This shot spends its whole budget on making the shared instrument provable.
Counterexample
none.
Formula reveal
none. Act A's readouts leave with the labels.
Camera move
Hold, then a slow push to the band and the ruler together, so both stay in the same shot and the measurement cannot be a cut.
Narration line
"Same gauge. Nothing has been calibrated, adjusted, or replaced."
Exit state
One amber band on a rail, tagged GAUGE_SIGMA against a ruler. No factory, no job, no percentage.
Shot 02

Factory A: sort what looks the same

Beat 1
Persistent object
The GAUGE_SIGMA band, unchanged, now standing beside a sorting bin in a masked plant.
Learner prediction
Will this gauge separate two populations sitting this close together?
Transformation
Two part populations appear with their spread taken from A_PART. Each part is drawn twice: once as a truth mark, once as a reading offset by the amber band. The two sets of readings interleave.
Surprise
The truth marks are separable and the readings are not. The overlap is made of the amber band and nothing else in the shot.
Counterexample
none in this shot. Factory B is the counterexample and arrives next, so the contrast survives the cut.
Formula reveal
none. Beat 3 owns the first percentage, so no ratio is allowed here.
Camera move
Static on the bin. The interleaving readings are the only motion, so the overlap is not something the camera did.
Narration line
"This plant has to put parts in the right bin, and the parts are close together on purpose."
Exit state

Shot 03

Factory B: judge against the drawing

The same GAUGE_SIGMA band, carried across the cut without a resize, matched against the shot 01 ruler again.

Shot 04

Name the denominator first

Beat 3
Persistent object
Two copies of the same GAUGE_SIGMA band, one per factory, drawn at identical width in a split frame.
Learner prediction
Required before the shot resolves. The learner places Study variation or Tolerance under each factory while no percentage exists anywhere on screen.
Transformation
Each factory grows a fraction skeleton with the numerator already filled by its amber band and the denominator empty. The two candidate lengths hover beside the empty slot.
Surprise
The two numerators are identical, so nothing about this choice is a fact about the gauge. The learner is choosing a question.
Counterexample
Dropping Tolerance into Factory A's slot is shown as a legal move that produces a real number answering a question that plant never asked.
Formula reveal
Fraction skeletons only: the bar and the numerator GAUGE_SIGMA, with no value and no ratio name yet.
Camera move
Split hold, both factories in frame at once, so the choice is made side by side rather than remembered across a cut.
Narration line
"Before a percentage exists, say what you are dividing by."
Exit state
Chosen denominator = Study variation under Factory A and Chosen denominator = Tolerance under Factory B. Percentages still hidden.
Shot 05

Opposite actions, one instrument

Beat 4
Persistent object
The same GAUGE_SIGMA band, drawn once and fed into all four cells, so one numerator is visibly shared.
Learner prediction
Which cells earn use and which earn replace, before the ratios resolve.
Transformation
A grid fills in. Rows are the two factories, columns are the two jobs. Cells resolve to study_ratio(part=A_PART) and tolerance_ratio(tolerance=A_TOL) for Factory A, then study_ratio(part=B_PART) and tolerance_ratio(tolerance=B_TOL) for Factory B. Each band comes from verdict() against ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT, and each band names its action.
Surprise
Hold the job fixed and move the same gauge from one plant to the other, and the action flips between replace and use. Neither the gauge nor the gate line moved during the flip.
Counterexample
The unasked cell in each row. Each plant's gauge earns the opposite action for the question that plant did not ask, which is why the answer cannot live inside the instrument. The generous denominator is Tolerance in Factory A and Study variation in Factory B, so there is no denominator to memorise.
Formula reveal
Both ratios printed for the first time in the act, with the shared numerator drawn as one band branching into four cells.
Camera move
One pull-back to hold all four cells, then no further move. The flip is shown by swapping the highlighted cell, not by travelling the camera.
Narration line
"Both of these are honest. They are answers to different questions."
Exit state
Four filled cells, use in teal and replace in alarm, the band between ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT still empty and still uncoloured.
Shot 06

ndc is the same curve

Beat 5
Persistent object
The GAUGE_SIGMA band, now the fixed denominator inside every point that gets plotted, held in the corner at its shot 01 width.
Learner prediction
Can ndc and %GRR_study ever disagree about the same gauge?
Transformation
One curve is drawn from ndc_from_study_ratio, with %GRR_study on the horizontal axis. Both factory points, computed the long way through ndc(gauge=GAUGE_SIGMA, part=A_PART) and ndc(gauge=GAUGE_SIGMA, part=B_PART), drop onto the curve and land on it exactly, which is NDC_IS_REDUNDANT.
Surprise
The curve falls monotonically, so ndc cannot disagree with %GRR_study. It is the same ratio rearranged, and NDC_K is the only thing added.
Counterexample
The two printed gates are not the same line. The vertical at REJECT_PCT and the horizontal at NDC_MIN meet the curve at different places, separated by NDC_GATE_GAP. The NDC_MIN crossing sits at study_ratio_for_ndc(NDC_MIN), reported as STUDY_PCT_AT_NDC5, which lands inside the band between ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT, where the action is improve.
Formula reveal
ndc_from_study_ratio as the curve itself and study_ratio_for_ndc as the crossing. NDC_K appears as the constant it is, without a derivation, because the shot's claim is about redundancy rather than about the constant.
Camera move
Slow zoom into the gap between the two gate lines, ending with both gates and the curve still in frame.
Narration line
"One of these two printed rules is stricter than the other, and the table they share does not say so."
Exit state
One curve, both factory points sitting on it, and three printed gates landed on it: ACCEPT_PCT, REJECT_PCT, and NDC_MIN. The action inside the middle band reads improve in a neutral tile with no third colour.
Shot 07

The chain

Beat 6
Persistent object
The GAUGE_SIGMA band, unchanged, parked beside the chain as the one thing that never becomes a link in it.
Learner prediction
Which link is the only place a person makes a choice?
Transformation
Five tokens fly in from where they were earned and lock into place: Decision job from shots 02 and 03, Chosen denominator from shot 04, Computed %GRR from shot 05, AIAG band from ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT, and the action from that band.
Surprise
Only the first link was a decision. Everything after it is arithmetic and a lookup, and the amber band never joins the chain at all.
Counterexample
Swap only the Decision job token between the two factories. The last two links change with nothing else on screen touched, which is the whole act in one gesture.
Formula reveal
No new formula. Both ratios collapse into the single Computed %GRR slot that the chosen denominator selected.
Camera move
Pull back to full width once the chain is complete, then stop.
Narration line
"The gauge does not own a verdict. The job chooses the denominator, and the denominator carries the rest."
Exit state
The five-link chain held on screen with the unchanged band beside it, handing off to the challenge, which reuses these four field labels exactly.

Beat 6, exact labels

The chain the challenge shares

These four labels are the same strings the challenge and its wrong-answer feedback use, so a learner who saw the act can name the field the page names.

  • Decision jobsort close process populations or judge drawing conformance. The only link a person chooses.
  • Chosen denominatorStudy variation or Tolerance. Fixed by the job above it.
  • Computed %GRRstudy_ratio or tolerance_ratio, whichever the denominator selected. Read at printed precision.
  • AIAG bandPlaced by verdict() against ACCEPT_PCT and REJECT_PCT.
  • Actionuse, improve, or replace. The plant instruction the whole chain existed to produce.

Standing rules for the cut

Checks the Manim work inherits

These are acceptance conditions on the rendered act, not suggestions for the animator.

Muted read

With audio off, a reviewer must be able to say: the same band went to both plants, the job chose what it was divided by, and the action flipped without the instrument changing. Any shot that needs the voice track to make that link is rejected.

One number path

Every geometry and readout comes from msalab.against_what: GAUGE_SIGMA, A_PART, A_TOL, B_PART, B_TOL, study_ratio, tolerance_ratio, verdict, ACCEPT_PCT, REJECT_PCT, NDC_MIN, NDC_K, ndc, ndc_from_study_ratio, study_ratio_for_ndc, STUDY_PCT_AT_NDC5, NDC_GATE_GAP, and NDC_IS_REDUNDANT. No value is typed into the scene.

Colour policy

use keeps the teal accent and replace keeps the alarm accent. improve gets neutral ink and an unfilled tile, in the act and in the page, because it names the next action rather than a result.

Gate

Act B is locked when all six required beats appear in this order, no percentage precedes the denominator ask in shot 04, ndc and both printed gates share the single curve in shot 06, the middle band carries no third colour, and the shot 07 labels match the challenge strings character for character.
